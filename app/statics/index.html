Passkey playground

Minimal WebAuthn test page wired to the existing /api/sso/v1 auth APIs. Assumes you are already logged in (cookies present).

URLs used: GET /api/sso/v1/credentials/passkey,
POST /api/sso/v1/credentials/passkey,
GET /api/sso/v1/auth/passkey,
POST /api/sso/v1/auth/passkey.
Adjust below if your routing differs.
